title: "Rooted in Growth: Using Tree Planting Data to Analyze Urban Expansion in Edmonton"
excerpt: "This project explored how municipal tree planting records could be used to examine patterns of urban development in Edmonton. Using data from the City of Edmonton's Open Data portal, I designed and populated a relational MySQL database containing over one million records. I organized the data into interconnected tables and developed SQL queries to examine planting activity across neighbourhoods and time periods. By comparing planting dates and the distribution of trees across neighbourhoods, I investigated how tree planting patterns reflected the development of newer residential communities and the growth of Edmonton over time. The project combined relational database design, data preparation and SQL-based analysis to explore how an existing municipal dataset could be used to answer broader questions about urban development.
